Thread — free Bootstrap 5 single-product DTC template by uiCookies
=================================================================

Concept
-------
"Thread" is a fictional direct-to-consumer brand that makes exactly one product:
The Field Pack, a 20-litre everyday backpack. Where the sibling e-commerce
template (Shopfront) is a busy multi-product boutique, Thread is the opposite —
a story-driven, editorial single-product page with one confident buy path. It
mirrors how real DTC brands (Away, Bellroy, Peak Design) sell: cinematic hero,
a problem/solution narrative, alternating feature rows, a full spec sheet, an
honest comparison table, verified reviews, a lifetime-guarantee band, an FAQ,
and a sticky buy bar that follows you down the page. One page, no chrome,
vanilla JavaScript, inline-SVG icons, no jQuery.

Palette
-------
Ink        #1B1D1A  warm near-black — text, footer, guarantee & buy bar
Paper      #FAFAF7  warm off-white page canvas (deliberately NOT cream)
Stone      #E9E5DC  warm-grey tint for alternating sections & cards
Pine       #2F5D50  deep muted pine green — the single brand accent, all CTAs
Pine deep  #234A40  hover state
Sand       #B9A98A  warm signal for dark-panel eyebrows & swatch
Line       #D8D3C7  hairline borders

The identity is quiet-luxury: a hued warm-neutral system carrying almost the
whole page, with one restrained pine green doing all the accent work. It avoids
the cream+serif+terracotta cliché (paper is cooler, accent is green not clay)
and is deliberately calmer than Shopfront's energetic cobalt-and-flame retail
palette.

Type pairing
------------
Display : Fraunces (300-600, incl. italic) — a characterful old-style serif that
          gives the brand an editorial, premium voice in headlines and prices.
Body/UI : Hanken Grotesk (400-700) — a clean, legible humanist grotesque for
          body copy, labels, spec tables and buttons.
Two families only — the restraint is part of the "considered" story. Fonts load
from Google Fonts; Bootstrap 5 is self-hosted in css/vendor & js/vendor. All
icons are hand-written inline SVG. Footer carries "© 2026" and
"Free template by uiCookies".
